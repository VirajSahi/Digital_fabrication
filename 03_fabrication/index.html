However the biggest issue I faced was attaching the gear to the motor. My aim was to make it detachable, so I attached a nut to the center of my gear and epoxied a scre to my gear shaft. However, the fit wasn't tight enough, and the gear kept screwing itself off rather than moving with the shaft. Another issue I faced was the imperfect fit of many of my pieces, due to a miscalculation of the wood thickness. I had to chisel each tooth by hand individually to have it fit.
